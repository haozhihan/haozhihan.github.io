Hi! I am **Haozhi Han** (韩昊知), a Ph.D. student at the [School of Computer Science](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/), [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. Yifeng Chen and Assistant Prof. Kun Li. I am also a research intern at [Microsoft Research Asia](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/).

My research centers on **high-performance systems and algorithms for scalable physical intelligence — from microscopic science to embodied AI**. I am interested in rearchitecting scientific computing (e.g., stencil computations and quantum chemistry) to scale on modern AI accelerators such as Tensor Core units and GPUs.

My work has appeared at top venues in high-performance and parallel computing, including **PPoPP** and **SC** (with an **SC 2025 Best Student Paper** nomination). Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:haozhi.han@stu.pku.edu.cn).
